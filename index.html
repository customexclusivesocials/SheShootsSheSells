Questions, answered.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The free 1-on-1 call is where we talk through your goals and decide whether the launch kit is right for you. You only pay if you choose to move forward.

Do you take a share of my earnings?

No. Every offer is a flat fee, and you keep 100% of what you earn.

Do I keep control of my accounts?

Yes. Your accounts are in your name, and we set everything up and hand it over to you.

Which offer is right for me?

New to creating? Start with the launch kit. Prefer to learn and do it yourself? Grab the guide for your type of content: social media or 18+. Want a steady supply of content without shooting it all yourself? Get the AI twin.

Is the AI twin safe and consensual?

Your twin is built only from your own likeness, after identity verification and your written consent. You approve content before it is posted, and we follow each platform's AI labeling rules. You can cancel anytime and ask us to delete your data.

What do I need to provide for my AI twin?

Clear photos of your face and body, a short video of you talking and moving, and notes on your look and style. Your twin is ready in 7 days or less once we have everything, so the faster you send it, the faster you launch.
