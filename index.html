Questions, answered.

Which path is right for me?

Book the free 1-on-1 call. If you're new, we'll scope your setup. If you already have an account, we'll recommend a review, the inbox trial or full management.

Is the AI creator twin safe and consensual?

Your twin is built only from your own likeness, after identity verification and your written consent. You approve everything before it is posted, and content follows each platform's AI labeling rules. You can end the service and have your twin data deleted at any time.

How do you promote my account without shadowbans?

We never post adult links directly on TikTok or Instagram. Profiles sit in SFW niches like fashion, fitness and cosplay, and traffic moves through neutral bridge pages using compliant wording.

How does the 14-day pilot work?

Our female chat team takes over your DMs for 14 days using dedicated, secure access. We match your tone and use open-loop PPV scripting, and you track earnings live.

Who answers my subscribers?

Only trained female chatters based in Ontario. Before going live we hold a 30-minute session to capture your vocabulary, emojis and boundaries.

How is my identity and location kept private?

Credentials live in an encrypted password vault, accounts are accessed through static Canadian residential IPs, and geo-blocking protects your local identity.
